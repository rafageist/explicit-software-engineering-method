# Glossary

\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{description}
\item[\textbf{Artifact}] A produced, versioned, and inspectable work product that affects system behavior and can be validated and traced to intent or decisions.
\item[\textbf{Assumption}] A statement treated as true for reasoning or planning when evidence is missing or deferred, and that must remain identifiable until validated or invalidated.
\item[\textbf{Compensation}] An explicit and auditable acceptance of risk due to a missing required artifact or validation, recorded without removing the underlying obligation.
\item[\textbf{Contextual Parameters}] Declared inputs that characterize the engineering context and shape the required level of explicitness and validation for a given scope.
\item[\textbf{Decision}] A committed engineering choice among alternatives, made explicit and linked to the intent it serves.
\item[\textbf{Diagnosability}] The ability to explain a failure in method terms using explicit evidence of intents, decisions, assumptions, validations, compensations, and parameters.
\item[\textbf{Explicit}] Engineering reasoning captured as traceable artifacts with stated intent and validation rather than remaining undocumented or implicit.
\item[\textbf{Explicitness}] The property of engineering work being made explicit in a form that is traceable, evaluable, and diagnosable.
\item[\textbf{Failure}] An observed deviation from intended or required behavior or constraints that can be analyzed against explicit engineering reasoning.
\item[\textbf{Intent}] The desired outcome, property, or constraint that a change or system must satisfy, with a defined scope.
\item[\textbf{Method}] A prescriptive and executable set of obligations and constraints over engineering artifacts and relationships, parameterizable and analyzable, and independent of workflow order.
\item[\textbf{Method Application}] The concrete application of the method to a specific scope with declared parameters and explicit artifacts such that compliance and diagnosability can be evaluated.
\item[\textbf{Normative Rule}] A mandatory statement of obligation that defines what must be explicit and the consequences of omission.
\item[\textbf{Orthogonality}] Independence from workflow order or lifecycle sequence while maintaining fixed engineering obligations.
\item[\textbf{Process}] An ordered sequence of activities or phases used to organize work, which may embed a method but does not define engineering obligations by itself.
\item[\textbf{Software}] Executable code, configurations, and data that produce system behavior.
\item[\textbf{Software Product}] Software whose behavior, constraints, and purpose are accountable through explicit intent, decisions, validation, and traceability.
\item[\textbf{Traceability}] Explicit and navigable links among intents, decisions, assumptions, artifacts, and validations that allow reasoning to be inspected and evaluated.
\item[\textbf{Validation}] Evidence that an artifact or decision satisfies its stated intent under the declared context.
\end{description}
\end{multicols}
